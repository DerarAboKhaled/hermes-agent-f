Hermes Agent F

Hermes Agent F is a private, single-user personal assistant run by its owner on his own computer. It uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Contacts) only to act on the owner's own Google account at the owner's request, for example reading and sending his e-mail and storing encrypted backups in his own Google Drive.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
